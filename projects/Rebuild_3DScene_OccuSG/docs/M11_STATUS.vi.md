# M11 preparation — native local smoke

## Phạm vi

Đây là hai smoke local chuẩn bị M11: canonical point-cloud regression và synthetic RGBDFrame depth-channel sequence. Chưa phải nghiệm thu Kaggle/M11, simulator capture, Habitat hay Webots.

## Đường chạy

- `NativePointCloudMappingStage` chuyển canonical world points thành `ScanPoint`, gọi persistent native OctoMap M2 rồi pinned C++ MapConverter M3; hai frame dùng `full_refresh` và `partial_update`.
- `NativeRGBDMappingStage` dùng trực tiếp M1 `backproject_depth` và valid mask, rồi cùng persistent M2/M3 path; không có backprojection implementation thứ hai.
- `DudeM5RegionStage` nhận `FreeSpaceGrid2D` native và gọi worker DuDe/FIST local cùng `RegionUpdateProcessor` M5. Regions đưa vào builder chỉ là output native DuDe/M5; không có room polygon/region GT trong `FramePacket`.
- Builder chạy M6 object association với một detection cached có provenance, M7 benchmark navigation config, M8 membership/RoomNode và M9 graph commit. M10 reload/replay/trajectory/PNG chạy sau flush.

## RGB-D contract path — synthetic depth smoke (2026-09-28)

Script: `python3 scripts/run_native_rgbd_smoke.py --output artifacts/m11_rgbd_synthetic_smoke`.

- `NativeRGBDMappingStage` calls the existing M1 `backproject_depth` kernel with `RGBDFrame.depth_m`, intrinsics, `T_W_Copt`, and `valid_mask`, then passes its canonical world hit cloud through the same persistent native OctoMap M2 and C++ MapConverter M3 workers. The input metadata declares metres, axial depth, scale and limits. The synthetic source is uint16 millimetres decoded with M1 `decode_depth_to_meters`.
- The deterministic two-view fixture ray-casts against a room shell, floor, ceiling and box obstacle. Base pose changes from (0.55, 0, 0) to (1.10, 0.18, 0), yaw from 0 to 0.14 rad. Camera origin comes from sensor transform including mount offset; recorded origins are (0.65, 0, 1.2) and approximately (1.199, 0.194, 1.2).
- RGB is intentionally absent: this tests the RGBDFrame depth channel and makes no color-image or simulator-capture claim. Each frame has 3,072 pixels; 3,048 finite, 3,040 valid-mask pixels and 3,017 M1-projected hits. NaNs and mask-invalid samples are omitted; M2 receives exactly 3,017 hit points and zero no-return rays.
- M2 has both free and occupied keys on both frames (frame 0: 113,097 free / 2,754 occupied; frame 1: 79,343 free / 2,534 occupied). Native M3 has free and occupied cells; projection runs `full_refresh` then `partial_update`.
- DuDe/M5 returns one region on each frame; both updates are `success` and accepted, branches `accept_incremental` then `accept_reuse_adjusted_no_differential`, stale=false. Final graph has 28 nodes, 156 edges, revision 3, one RoomNode and two agent poses with 0.579 m displacement. Snapshot reload and delta replay match; graph has no dangling/self edges.
- Timings are named by scope: startup 13.22 ms; depth fixture generation 197.39 ms; builder update/flush/close 2,977.67 ms; export/render/reload 1,555.80 ms. Python process `ru_maxrss` high-water was 195,896 KiB; child RSS maximum sampled after frames/flush was 32,656 KiB. These local measurements are not a benchmark.
- Artifacts include snapshot/deltas, trajectory CSV/JSON, world-coordinate PNGs, per-frame `diagnostics.json` and manifest. GT regions are absent; one cached synthetic detection is provenance-tagged.

Point-cloud smoke and this synthetic depth-channel smoke are two separate local regressions. Neither is a simulator capture. Simulator capture and Kaggle benchmark remain separate future stages. No cloud upload occurred; M4 license/distribution gates remain unresolved.

## Layered diagnosis: why this fixture ends with one region (2026-09-28)

Runner/artifacts: `scripts/diagnose_two_room_sequence.py`, `artifacts/m11_two_room_diagnostics/`. The original sequence artifacts/config remain untouched. Capture checkpoints are frames 13 (before door), 16 (just through), 22 (observe room 2), 34 (return), and 43 (final revisit); each stores gzip-compressed native M2 leaf snapshot, M3 values/heights/revision/origin/resolution, opt-in M4 cleaned and processed-occupied masks, raw M4 responses/contours, M5 output, and stderr from sequential/fresh workers. PNG overlays use world coordinates and show fixture geometry only as an evaluation overlay. The separate ideal-grid input is never sent into the exploration builder.

- **A — fresh final grid still yields one region.** Final partial-update grid is 177×81, 0.05 m, origin `(0, -2)`, M3 revision 44. With unchanged M4 profile (`decomp_threshold=1.5 m`, `safety_distance=0.5 m`), a new DuDe worker returns one region. Its actual preprocessing has 13,184 free pixels and 1,125 processed occupied/black pixels. Source diagnostics show 2 contours in the wrapper hierarchy (one hole), one valid polygon, `cuts_after_resolution=0`, and one output region. This rules out “the persistent worker alone caused the final one-region count.”
- **First observed structural mismatch is at M2→M3 projection, not M8.** M2 has occupied leaves in the partition-neighbour slab at final frame (1,175 upper-jamb and 233 lower-jamb occupied leaves for `|x−4.4|≤0.12 m`, outside the doorway Y interval, `0≤z≤2.4 m`; this is a broad slab diagnostic, not one-to-one voxel parity). Yet in the final M3 partition band `x∈[4.2,4.6)`, outside doorway `|y|≤1.1`, only 12/288 cells are occupied, while 194 are free and 82 unknown. The M4 cleaned mask retains 59 free pixels in the upper-jamb portion of this band (0 in lower jamb); the corresponding ideal-grid preprocessing retains 0 in both jamb portions. Thus the projected/preprocessed wall barrier is visibly less continuous than the fixture geometry. This localizes the first *observed* loss to the M2→M3 representation boundary, while the exact cause (coverage/leaf placement versus projection/callback semantics) remains open.
- **Partial-update is not the sole explanation.** After the 44-frame run, a diagnostic full-refresh was made from the same accumulated M2 OcTree. Compared with final partial-update, 301/14,337 common M3 cells differ (partial: 13,738 free/119 occupied/480 unknown; full-refresh: 13,658/260/419). Full-refresh improves the jamb band only to 20 occupied/194 free/74 unknown; fresh DuDe still returns one region (2 wrapper contours, one hole, zero cuts). This is a diagnostic control, not a baseline reset/fallback. It shows both projection modes produce a mask that remains merged under this fixture/profile.
- **Isolated ideal-geometry control.** A GT-coded occupancy grid at the exact final shape/resolution/origin, encoding shell, full partition except doorway, and the two fixture obstacles, was sent only to a fresh DuDe diagnostic worker. Under the same M4 parameters, preprocessing has 9,582 free and 4,578 processed occupied pixels; wrapper sees 3 contours (two holes), invokes two cuts, and returns 2 regions. This demonstrates that pinned DuDe can yield two regions on this idealized mask with the selected profile; it is not evidence that RGB-D reconstruction or the actual M3 grid should match it.
- **B — sequential/fresh divergence starts at frame 1 (M3 revision 2).** Frame 0 outputs are identical. At frame 1 both workers are `success/accept_incremental` and return two regions, but their split boundary differs: sequential bounds split at x≈4.65 m; fresh at x≈4.20 m. Rasterized union IoU is 0.9825 (90 differing cells); the pairwise/raw ordering and per-region geometry differ. At final frame, sequential uses `accept_reuse_adjusted_no_differential` and does not invoke the kernel; fresh uses `accept_incremental`, invokes it, and both return one region (union IoU 0.9735). Every frame 1–43 has non-identical serialized geometry. Frame 12 must be treated separately: sequential backend rejects/preserves previous while fresh accepts a newly decomposed candidate, so those outputs do not represent equivalent branches. M5 marks that sequential update stale and does not refresh accepted evidence.
- The ideal grid was the only input containing GT-coded wall/room geometry, and it was not used for M5–M9. No DuDe kernel, baseline parameters, room promotion rules, or reference sequence were changed. No automatic reset/fallback was added. Cloud upload remains false; M4 rights/cloud gates remain open.
- Focused regression after opt-in mask diagnostics: `PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m pytest -q tests/test_m4_dude.py tests/test_m4_source_parity.py` — **10 passed**. Diagnostic replay ran 44 frames and emitted all listed artifacts; `python3 -m py_compile scripts/diagnose_two_room_sequence.py` passed. Full suite was **not rerun in this diagnostic turn**.

**Current diagnosis, not a fix:** the earliest clear evidence is that the accumulated M2 occupancy is not represented as a continuous partition in M3, and this remains true after same-tree full-refresh. The first confirmed segmentation consequence is that M4 sees one outer contour with one hole and performs zero cuts on the final actual grid. Further work should compare exact partition-crossing M2 leaves against M3 cell/height decisions and pinned MapConverter callback inputs, then decide whether the observed limitation is coverage, source projection behavior, or adapter parity. Any changed-coverage route or extended decomposition policy must be reported separately from the unchanged reference baseline.
## M2→M3 diagnostic addendum: same-tree controls and pinned-source parity (2026-09-28)

This addendum supersedes the earlier interpretation of the broad 288-cell slab: that count was not wall recall, and it did not establish M2→M3 as the first failure point.

Artifacts: `artifacts/m11_two_room_diagnostics_m3_controls/diagnostics.json`; per-column trace with leaf AABBs and source height intervals: `milestones/final_column_traces.json`; one-voxel ideal control: `milestones/ideal_voxel_footprint_comparison.json`.

- **A/B/C same accumulated OcTree.** A is final partial output; B is full-refresh on the already-live converter; C constructs a new ProjectionBackend/MapConverter and feeds it the exact current OcTree leaves (count 408,146, preserving leaf centers, float32 half-size, and occupancy state; no scan reinsertion). A has 81×177 cells, B 81×177, C 81×176. On aligned overlap, B/C have 0 categorical mismatches, 6 floor and 6 ceiling mismatches; both fresh DuDe probes return one region. Thus MapConverter history does not explain the one-region count in this fixture. A differs from B in 301 categorical cells, 21 floor and 4 ceiling values; A/C differ in 221 categorical cells, 27 floor and 10 ceiling values. These are update-mode/state observations, not proof that either output is more correct.
- **Direct pinned MapConverter oracle through the live sequence.** A separate C++ oracle keeps one pinned MapConverter alive over all 44 revisions and independently prepares the callback bounds/leaves from the same OcTree history. At every revision, changed bounds and exact input-leaf fingerprint/count match the runtime adapter; all occupancy values, floor heights and ceiling heights match (zero mismatches). Clean C also matches a newly constructed direct pinned MapConverter on the same tree. This supports callback/backend parity for this route and rules out an observed M3 adapter mismatch here; it is not parity for all inputs or the whole pipeline.
- **Column/AABB trace.** Samples are Map2D lattice samples `origin + index×0.05 m`; Map2D indexes with `floor((world-offset)/resolution)`. Exact AABB inventory finds 156 occupied and 1,115 free leaves intersecting the wall plane, jamb area and z∈[0,2.4] m; this is also not recall.
- The selected exact-source-formula examples are recorded with contributing leaf AABBs/states, float32 half-size, Z intervals and union/clearance results. At the occupied sample (x=4.40,y=−1.45), 54 occupied intervals have no surviving free interval; a west-adjacent free interval overlaps them and source `getOccupation` yields 100, matching the M3 occupied value. At the jamb free sample (4.40,1.45), the source union has 92 free and 100 occupied raw intervals; one free interval survives the >0.3 m filter, giving free=0 and heights ≈0.04995–2.35005 m. The unknown sample (4.40,−1.70) has 19 occupied intervals, no surviving free interval and no qualifying neighbor score; M3 is unknown. Doorway sample (4.40,0) retains a free interval and remains free. The complete inputs/results are in the trace artifact.
- **Ideal raster audit.** The earlier predicate `abs(x−4.4)≤0.05` marks three lattice columns (4.35, 4.40, 4.45): 0.10 m center span / 0.15 m nominal cell-bin width. A separate diagnostic one-column footprint marks only x=4.40 (0.05 m nominal width); fresh DuDe still returns two regions. The idealized two-region result therefore does not require the earlier three-column thickness, but it still does not prove the reconstructed grid ought to split.

Coverage/raster alignment and source projection semantics remain possible interacting factors; these results do not isolate one unique cause. No M4 input, baseline parameters, DuDe kernel, automatic reset, or M8 policy was changed.
